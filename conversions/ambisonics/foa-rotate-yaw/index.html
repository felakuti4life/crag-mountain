Algorithm Description

FOA Transform: Rotate (Yaw) — Rotate the Soundfield Around the Z Axis Applies a horizontal yaw rotation to a First-Order Ambisonics (FOA) stream. A positive angle rotates the soundfield counterclockwise when viewed from above (i.e. sources appear to shift towards the left). Transform (ACN/SN3D, rotation of soundfield by angle ψ around Z axis): W′ = W Y′ = Y·cos(ψ) + X·sin(ψ) Z′ = Z X′ = X·cos(ψ) − Y·sin(ψ) Derivation: a source at azimuth θ is mapped to θ+ψ. Expanding sin(θ+ψ) and cos(θ+ψ) using angle-addition identities gives the matrix above. Parameters: yaw – rotation angle in radians. Positive = counterclockwise from above. Channel count: 4 (subtype="ambisonics") → 4 (subtype="ambisonics") References: Henderson, J. & Thornburg, H. (2011). The Ambisonic Toolkit: A tiled approach to B-format composition and analysis. ICMC 2011. https://ambisonictoolkit.net/ Zotter, F. & Frank, M. (2019). Ambisonics. Springer. §3.
